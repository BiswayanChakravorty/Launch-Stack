# LaunchStack MVP outcomes

## Outcome 1 — Spreadsheet-backed 250-platform catalog
- The product uses the supplied `startup-launch-directories.csv.xlsx` as its catalog source of truth.
- The catalog exposes 250 rows with platform name, website link, and Free/Freemium/Paid access label.
- The UI supports platform search, result count, access filtering, direct links, and a compact platform list.

## Outcome 2 — Founder launch intake
- A founder can enter product name, one-line idea, website link, target audience, positioning, and homepage screenshot.
- The screenshot appears as a thumbnail in the launch kit summary.
- The campaign state is saved locally so the founder can return without paying for a backend.

## Outcome 3 — Channel-specific launch scripts
- The product generates deterministic, editable launch copy from the founder inputs.
- The workspace shows platform-specific scripts for Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, Indie Hackers, and additional catalog platforms.
- Each script includes the correct destination platform link and a copy action.

## Outcome 4 — Packaging and pricing
- The landing page communicates the 10-platform free tier, 30-platform $29 tier, and 50+ platform $57.99 tier.
- A founder can select a plan to update the launch kit scope in the local workspace.
- The UI clearly distinguishes free, freemium, and paid submission destinations.

## Outcome 5 — Launch-ready marketing surface
- The home page clearly explains the LaunchStack motto: launch once, adapt everywhere.
- The page includes a founder-oriented CTA, catalog quantity proof, workflow explanation, and a path from free launch to paid campaign.
- The first release does not require a paid API or account backend.
